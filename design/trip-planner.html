How each part is built

The data

Open-Meteo Archive (ERA5, free, 1940→now, hourly). Has every field the engine needs; the one gap — precip probability — we map from whether it actually rained. ~10 fetches per place+month, cached in the browser.

The scoring

Reuse evaluateDay unchanged, once per historical day, on the absolute scale (so places/months compare fairly). Per-day averages → the calendar; the whole month → the ring + odds; every day's safety flags → Heads-up.

The build

A new Plan tab + #/plan route, client-only, no backend. Reuses the ring, distribution, metric rows, and the character-phrase voice. Phase 2: “this year vs typical” + compare two places.
